Troubleshooting (90% of issues)

Microphone button does nothing Chrome → padlock icon in the address bar → allow Microphone. Must be Chrome/Edge.

No sound Click anywhere on the page once, then ask again (browsers block audio before the first interaction).

Page looks outdated after a change Hard refresh: Cmd+Shift+R (Mac) / Ctrl+Shift+R (Windows).

"model not configured" You didn’t paste your key into config.json, or you left the placeholder text.

Answers are generic The notes folder path was wrong in Prompt 1 — run it again with the full absolute path.

Anything else Paste the exact error into your coding assistant and say "fix it." That's the whole method.
