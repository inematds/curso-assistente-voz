How it works

Two AIs debate. You pick the winner.

Two AIs debate. You pick the winner. This package is the framework for my JARVIS vs TARS debate show: the same two personalities, the same rules of engagement, running as text in any AI chat. It takes five minutes and is genuinely funny. The final app I use on camera does this out loud with two real voices, but this beginner version is yours for free and works tonight.

1 Open a new chat. The assistant works best for the back-and-forth, and the free plan is enough. Other assistants work too.

2 Paste the Mega Prompt from the next page, exactly as written. The show introduces itself.

3 Give them a topic. Type your own or pick one from the Topic Bank. The discussion starts instantly.

4 Jump in at any time. Type your opinion in the middle of the discussion and both pause for you. Type VERDICTS when you’ve heard enough, then crown your winner.

WHY THE SHOW WORKS

JARVIS argues for the long game with devastating manners. TARS argues for the mission in eight direct words. They genuinely can't stand each other, and the rules force every line to carry a real example, mechanism, or number. The rivalry seasons the debate. The reasoning wins it.
